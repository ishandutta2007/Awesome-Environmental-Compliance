# Awesome-Environmental-Compliance

## Top Environmental Compliance Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**
*Focused on EHS Management, Regulatory Tracking, Emissions Monitoring & Sustainability Compliance*
**Last updated: September 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Environmental Compliance**. These tools help organizations manage environmental, health, and safety (EHS) obligations, track regulatory changes, monitor emissions, manage hazardous materials, and maintain audit-ready compliance records.

**Examples** include Enhesa, Sphera, Cority, VelocityEHS, EcoOnline, Enablon, ProcessMAP, Quentic, IsoMetrix, and Benchmark Gensuite (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project for self-hosting, custom compliance workflows, and transparent environmental data management — ideal for facilities managers, EHS professionals, and developers who need full control over sensitive safety and regulatory data without expensive per-facility subscriptions.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Enhesa](https://www.enhesa.com/)**  
  Global regulatory compliance intelligence platform. Tracks environmental, health, and safety regulations across 300+ jurisdictions, providing monitoring, gap analysis, and compliance guidance for multinational organizations.

- **[Sphera](https://sphera.com/)**  
  Integrated ESG and operational risk management platform. Covers environmental compliance, safety management, product stewardship, and sustainability reporting with deep regulatory content libraries.

- **[Cority](https://www.cority.com/)**  
  Enterprise EHS and sustainability software. Provides environmental compliance management, emissions tracking, waste management, and regulatory reporting with configurable workflows .

- **[VelocityEHS](https://www.velocityehs.com/)**  
  EHS and ESG software platform. Features chemical management, SDS authoring, environmental compliance, incident management, and safety training with mobile accessibility.

- **[EcoOnline](https://www.ecoonline.com/)**  
  EHS and chemical safety management platform. Provides COSHH assessments, SDS management, incident reporting, and environmental compliance tracking for industrial and healthcare organizations.

- **[Enablon](https://enablon.com/)**  
  Wolters Kluwer's integrated risk and compliance platform. Covers EHS, operational risk, and sustainability with regulatory content, emissions management, and audit management modules.

- **[ProcessMAP](https://www.processmap.com/)**  
  EHS and sustainability software (now part of Ideagen). Provides incident management, environmental compliance, risk assessment, and compliance calendar tools.

- **[Quentic](https://www.quentic.com/)**  
  EHS and CSR management software. Combines environmental compliance, occupational safety, and sustainability management with mobile apps for field data collection.

- **[IsoMetrix](https://isometrix.com/)**  
  Integrated risk management platform with EHS capabilities. Covers environmental compliance, sustainability reporting, and governance with configurable workflows for mining, energy, and industrial sectors.

- **[Benchmark Gensuite](https://benchmarkgensuite.com/)**  
  EHS and sustainability software suite. Provides environmental compliance tracking, permit management, emissions monitoring, and audit-ready record keeping for global enterprises.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[EcoComply AI](https://github.com/benaiahbrown/ecocomply-ai)**  
  AI-powered environmental compliance assistant combining RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) with real-time web search. Indexes 67 federal and state regulatory documents (~77k chunks) and performs gap analysis to flag when knowledge base needs updating. Multi-agent system with FastAPI, ChromaDB, LangChain, and Supabase. Real estate/landowner focused. ⚠️ Documentation only — source code in private repo .

- **[GreenCompliance AI](https://github.com/didagesierich/green-compliance-ai)**  
  Full-stack environmental compliance analysis tool. Analyzes business descriptions, detects compliance risks (waste disposal, emissions, hazardous materials), generates compliance scores (0-100), and maps issues to relevant regulations. Python Flask backend, SQLite database, rule-based regulation engine. Educational/research license .

- **[GuardianSDS](https://github.com/TAStagg/GuardianSDS)**  
  AI-native Safety Data Sheet (SDS) management and compliance platform. Moves safety documentation from "dead PDFs" into searchable, voice-activated safety engine. Features AI-neural extraction (PDF→structured JSON), Emergency Mode (one-tap access to First Aid/PPE without login), automated revision monitoring, GHS-compliant label generation, and offline-first caching. Next.js 16, PostgreSQL, Docker. MIT licensed .

- **[Open Source EHS Console](https://github.com/topics/safety-management)**  
  Self-hostable EHS web console for incidents, CAPA (Corrective and Preventive Actions), metrics, documents, training, and audits. Role-aware with PostgreSQL backend. TypeScript. Listed under GitHub's safety-management topic .

- **[rEHS](https://github.com/nadlerphd/rEHS)**  
  R package for EHS management. Set of tools for environmental health and safety data analysis and management. Apache licensed, version 0.1.0 from GitHub .

- **[JSAPY (Job Safety Analysis in Python)](https://ruc.udc.es/entities/publication/e438629c-3d3c-428c-9ade-3a191b002987/full)**  
  Open-source Python package for occupational risk prevention technicians. Three functional areas: work accident analysis, vibration exposure assessment (hand-arm and whole-body), and noise exposure analysis. Fully documented, tested with Pytest. Published as master's thesis from Universidade da Coruña .

- **[hashgraph/guardian](https://github.com/hashgraph/guardian)**  
  Open-source platform for creating, managing, and verifying digital environmental assets (carbon credits, renewable energy certificates). Customizable Policy Workflow Engine with Web3 technology for transparent, fraud-proof operations. TypeScript, Apache-2.0. 83 stars, 1,377 commits, active development .

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- **SDS & Chemical Management**: **GuardianSDS** (AI-powered SDS management with offline access), **Open Source EHS Console** (incident/CAPA/training management).
- **AI Compliance Assistants**: **EcoComply AI** (RAG + gap analysis), **GreenCompliance AI** (rule-based risk scoring).
- **Digital Environmental Assets**: **hashgraph/guardian** (carbon credits, RECs, Web3 verification).
- **EHS Data Analysis**: **rEHS** (R package), **JSAPY** (occupational risk calculations).

**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine **GuardianSDS** for SDS and chemical safety management, **Open Source EHS Console** for incident and CAPA tracking, **GreenCompliance AI** for regulatory gap analysis, and **PostgreSQL** for persistence. Add **Docker** for deployment and **Ollama** for self-hosted AI compliance assistance.
